# HexHaki 1.0.0 final balance pass

This pass intentionally avoids rewriting user-approved ability identities or cooldowns. It fixes progression gaps that were visible in the final source audit.

- ARM 400 Ryuo: emitted Armament attacks gain a real 1.08x milestone step.
- ARM 700 Internal Destruction: emitted Armament attacks reach a 1.16x milestone step.
- Advanced Haki Haki-Wave: additional 1.12x damage step, matching its 7s tap cooldown and late unlock.
- WiFi Haki range: 60 blocks at unlock, scaling to 160 at 1000 ARM/OBS.
- Full Galaxy Impact: release cost increased from 38 to 120 energy; 60s cooldown unchanged.
- Conqueror training: pressure-release units scale 1 -> 9 from tap -> MAX, so committed charges train more efficiently.
- Unlock thresholds, King's Grip curves, Last Stand, Observation projectile immunity, and persistent energy drains remain unchanged.
