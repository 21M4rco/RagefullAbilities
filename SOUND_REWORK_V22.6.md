# V22.6 Sound Rework

This build replaces every shipped HexHaki sound asset (37/37).

Design goals:
- Armament: dense hardening transient + electrical surface crackle.
- Observation: lighter focused cues with sharp danger alert.
- Ryuo: flowing charge movement followed by compressed release impact.
- Conqueror: deep pressure, violent crackle, wide release and sub-heavy detonations.
- Supreme/Convergence: longer cinematic rumble and air displacement.
- Galaxy Rage: separate absorb, charge, crack, punch and impact mixes with the largest dynamic profile in the bank.

Technical:
- 48 kHz, mono, OGG Vorbis.
- Normalized with headroom and short edge fades to avoid click artifacts.
- All 37 files regenerated for V22.6.
